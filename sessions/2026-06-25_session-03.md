## Key decisions made

### Stage 2 model: Clinical-Longformer (`yikuan8/Clinical-Longformer`)

Evaluated four candidate models for Stage 2:

| Model | Max tokens | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `emilyalsentzer/Bio_ClinicalBERT` (previous default) | 128 | Rejected — truncates most discharge notes |
| `medicalai/ClinicalBERT` | 256 | Rejected — DistilBERT-based, no published benchmarks |
| `yikuan8/Clinical-BigBird` | 4096 | Good, but loses to Longformer on every task |
| `yikuan8/Clinical-Longformer` | 4096 | **Selected** |

Decision based on the paper **Li et al. (2022), arXiv:2201.11838**
("Clinical-Longformer and Clinical-BigBird: Transformers for Long Clinical Sequences",
published as a journal article in JAMIA 2023):

- MIMIC-III discharge summaries average **1,435 words** — far beyond the 512-token BERT limit.
  Bio_ClinicalBERT silently discards the tail of every note (discharge plan, prognosis,
  follow-up instructions — exactly the signals that predict readmission).
- Clinical-Longformer wins on **all 10 benchmarks** in the paper (NER, QA, classification).
- On **MIMIC-AKI** — binary classification from MIMIC clinical notes, avg 1,463 tokens
  (closest available proxy to Stage 2's task):
  ClinicalBERT AUC 0.738 vs Clinical-Longformer AUC **0.762**, F1 0.444 vs **0.484**.
- The authors explicitly recommend Clinical-Longformer over Clinical-BigBird:
  *"Clinical-BigBird also requires more fine-tuning time; we recommend Clinical-Longformer
  for limited resources."*
- The paper explicitly cites readmission prediction as its primary motivation.
- Pre-trained on MIMIC-III clinical notes — same domain as this project.
- `max_seq_length` set to 1024 in config (model max is 4096) for practical MacBook fine-tuning.

### Stage 3 model: phi4-mini via Ollama

Evaluated Meditron-7B (`epfl-llm/meditron-7b`, EPFL) as a medical-domain candidate:

- Meditron-7B is a **base/foundation model — not instruction-tuned**. It continues text
  rather than following prompts, making it unsuitable for Stage 3's prompted generation task.
- Despite being medically pre-trained, instruction-tuned Zephyr-7B (63.3%) and Mistral-7B
  (60.0%) both beat it on MMLU-Medical (54.2%).
- Truthfulness score only 28.3% on medical categories.
- Authors themselves caution against deployment without extensive validation.
- Stage 3 is an **instruction-following + clear writing** task, not medical QA. A well-tuned
  small general model is a better fit than a large unaligned medical base model.
- **phi4-mini** selected: ~3.8B, instruction-tuned, strong reasoning, fast on Apple Silicon,
  available via Ollama (`ollama pull phi4-mini`).
