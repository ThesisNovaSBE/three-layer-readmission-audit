## Files changed

- `README.md` — full rewrite (structure, Stage 2 framing, prose style).
  See "Key decisions" above for the substance.
- `scripts/run_validation_controls.py` — added `flush=True` to every
  `print()` call. Without it, SLURM's file-redirected stdout buffers
  Python's output, and a process killed by `scancel` (not a clean exit)
  never flushes that buffer, so real progress never reaches the log
  file. This is exactly what made job 16873264 look stuck: it had
  actually completed all 150 D1 and 40 D2 patients, but none of the
  `[i/n]` checkpoint lines ever made it to disk before cancellation. The
  CSV data itself was never at risk, since the CSV writer was already
  flushed explicitly after every row.
