AUROC gap of 0.094 (best: 18-40, worst: 70+) is the main fairness finding — Longformer reads younger patients' notes better. 70+ notes likely contain more fragmented language (frailty, delirium, polypharmacy) that the model generalises from poorly. This directly motivates the `frailty_markers` and `cognition` categories in Stage 3. AUROC of 0.676 (text only) being slightly below Stage 1's 0.706 (structured) is expected and strengthens the thesis argument: neither modality dominates, so the cross-modal discordance analysis is genuinely motivated.
