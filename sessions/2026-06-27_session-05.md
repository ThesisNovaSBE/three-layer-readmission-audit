## Key decisions made

**Stage 2 training strategy:**
- Fine-tune Clinical-Longformer on a stratified 15k-note subsample (out of 251k available)
- Cap per-epoch eval to 3k notes so periodic checkpointing does not dominate wall time
- Reasoning: full 251k notes × 5 epochs × periodic eval = 46+ days on CPU; 15k ≈ 13 hours overnight
- 15k notes is ~6% of the full training set — representative, stratified, methodologically valid
- Report methodology clearly in thesis: sample size and motivation are part of the contribution

**max_seq_length stays at 512:**
- Clinical-Longformer supports 4096 tokens; MIMIC-III discharge notes average ~1900 tokens
- Bumping to 1024 would make per-step training ~11x slower (memory pressure on Apple Silicon)
- 512 tokens captures the beginning of discharge summaries where diagnosis/plan appear
- Noted as a limitation: full context window only feasible on GPU

**Threshold (Stage 2 confirmation):**
- Kept at default 0.5 — needs tuning after training completes
- Run `python -m src.stage2.predict --threshold X` to experiment with thresholds
